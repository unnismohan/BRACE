# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a straightforward, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being welcoming to people who are new to the project or to testing in general
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Assuming good faith — most disagreements are misunderstandings

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in project spaces — the repository, issues, merge requests,
discussions — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through a confidential GitLab
issue. Reports are reviewed and investigated, and will be handled in a way that
protects the reporter's privacy.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate.
